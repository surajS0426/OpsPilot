# Printer offline or jammed

runbook_id: printer-offline
category: hardware

## Symptoms
Print jobs do not come out and the printer shows as "offline" on the user's laptop, or the printer's own display shows a paper jam or error. Other printers usually still work.

## First checks
1. Check the printer's display for errors, paper jams or low toner.
2. Confirm the printer is switched on and its network cable is plugged in.
3. Ask whether other people can print to the same printer.

## Fix
1. Clear any paper jam using the diagram inside the printer's panels, removing torn pieces.
2. Turn the printer off, wait 30 seconds and turn it on again.
3. On the laptop, clear the print queue and print a test page.
4. Remove and re-add the printer using the name on its label.

## Escalate when
- Printer still offline for the whole team after all fix steps: assign to the desktop-support queue as P3.
- Printer shows a hardware fault code or keeps jamming: assign to the desktop-support queue as P3.
- One person cannot print to any printer: assign to the desktop-support queue as P4.
